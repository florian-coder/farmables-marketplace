## What we are, and what we are not

**We are an intermediary. We are never the seller.**

Each farm on this platform sells to you directly. The farm is the seller of the goods, issues the sale, and is responsible for the food it produces. We provide the marketplace, the payment processing, and the dispute handling, and we take a commission for it.

This matters when something goes wrong: your contract for the goods is with the farm. But you do not have to work out who to chase — **report the problem to us and we handle it**, including refunding you directly if that is what is fair.

Each farm's own details — legal name, registration number, registered office — are shown on its public page, as DSA art. 30 requires.

## If we cannot resolve it

You always have these routes, and using them costs you nothing:

- **ANPC** — the National Authority for Consumer Protection: <https://anpc.ro>
- **SAL** — alternative dispute resolution: <https://anpc.ro/ce-este-sal/>
- **SOL** — the EU online dispute resolution platform: <https://ec.europa.eu/consumers/odr>
- **ANSPDCP** — the data protection authority: <https://www.dataprotection.ro>

We will tell you about these routes rather than wait for you to find them.

## Producers

If you produce food and want to sell here, start at [Sign up](/auth/signup) and choose *producer*. You will need:

- a producer's certificate, or trade register registration, for fruit and vegetables
- a sanitary-veterinary authorisation for eggs
- a sanitary-veterinary authorisation for honey
- a Stripe account, which we will help you set up — **we never see or store your bank details**

Read the [Producer Terms](/legal/terms-producers) before you apply. In particular: how [ranking works](/legal/ranking), what our commission is, and how much notice we give before changing terms.
