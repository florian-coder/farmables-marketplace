# Returns, Cancellations and Refunds

This page explains, in plain terms, when you can cancel, when you can get your money back, and how long it takes.

The short version: **fresh food is legally exempt from the 14-day change-of-mind right, but every other protection you have still applies in full.** If what arrived is not what was described, is short, or is not fit to eat, you get your money back.

## 1. The 14-day withdrawal right, and why food is different

For most things bought online, OUG 34/2014 gives you 14 days to change your mind for no reason.

**Article 16 of the same law removes that right for goods that spoil quickly.** Vegetables, fruit, eggs, dairy and honey delivered fresh fall under this exemption. This is not our policy — it is the law, and it exists because a returned punnet of strawberries cannot be resold.

So: **you cannot return fresh food simply because you changed your mind.**

What follows is everything you *can* do.

## 2. Cancelling before the farm starts

You can cancel a paid order at no cost while it is still **awaiting the farm's acceptance** or has just been **accepted** and not yet prepared.

- Open **Orders**, find the order, and use **Cancel order**.
- The full amount — products, delivery and the platform service fee — goes back to your card.
- Stock returns to the farm's shelf automatically.

Once a farm has started preparing your order, the button disappears. Message the farm; many will accommodate you, but at that point it is their call, not an automatic right.

## 3. When the farm cancels

A farm may decline or cancel an order — a failed crop, a broken van, illness.

When that happens:

- You are refunded **in full and automatically**, including delivery and the platform service fee.
- We take the money back from the farm before it reaches you, so the farm carries the cost, not you.
- You get an email telling you it happened and what the farm said.

You never have to chase a refund for an order a farm cancelled. If you ever do, that is a bug — tell us.

## 4. When something is wrong with what arrived

This is where your rights are strongest, and they come from GO 21/1992 and Law 449/2003 rather than from us.

**Tell us within 48 hours of delivery** for anything perishable. That is not a legal deadline — it is how long the evidence lasts. Photographs help enormously.

You are entitled to a refund or a replacement if:

- **Something is missing** from what you were charged for
- **The quantity is short** beyond the tolerance in §6
- **The goods are not what was described** — a different variety, not the advertised grade, not organic when sold as organic
- **The goods are not fit to eat** on arrival
- **The order never arrived**

### How to report it

1. Open **Orders** and find the order.
2. Use **Report a problem** and describe what is wrong. Attach photographs if you can.
3. The farm has **48 hours** to respond.
4. If the farm does not respond, or you cannot agree, the case comes to us and we decide.

We can refund you directly, in full or in part, without the farm's agreement. We would rather the two of you sort it out — but you are not left waiting on someone who has stopped replying.

## 5. Full and partial refunds

**A full refund** returns products, delivery and the platform service fee.

**A partial refund** — for example, two of five kilos missing — returns:

- the value of the missing products, plus
- the same proportion of the platform service fee.

**The delivery fee is not refunded on a partial refund**, because a van still drove to your door with the rest of the order. If *nothing* usable arrived, that is a full refund and delivery comes back too.

We never keep our service fee on money you did not get value for.

## 6. Weight, and why it is not always exact

Fresh produce cannot be portioned to the gram. A "1 kg" bag of tomatoes is what the farm weighed out, and it will not be exactly 1,000 g.

**We allow a tolerance of ±10% on any item sold by weight.**

- **Within 10%:** the price stands. You may get slightly more or slightly less. This is normal and it evens out.
- **More than 10% short:** you are refunded the difference automatically, without having to ask.
- **More than 10% over:** you are **not** charged extra. The farm gave you more than it billed you for, and that is on them.

Your card is charged once, at the price shown at checkout. **We never charge you again after the fact**, whatever the scales say. A payment you did not authorise is not a payment we are willing to take.

## 7. How long a refund takes

| Stage | Typical time |
|---|---|
| We start the refund | Immediately, when the cancellation is confirmed |
| Stripe sends it to your bank | Same day |
| It appears on your statement | 5–10 business days |

That last step is your bank's, not ours. If more than 10 business days have passed, contact us with the order number and we will trace it.

Refunds always go back **to the card you paid with**. We cannot send them anywhere else — that is a card network rule, and it is what stops a stolen card being turned into cash.

## 8. If you are still not happy

1. **Message the farm.** Most problems end here.
2. **Report it to us.** We read every report.
3. **ANPC.** The National Authority for Consumer Protection: <https://anpc.ro>
4. **SAL** — alternative dispute resolution, free for consumers: <https://anpc.ro/ce-este-sal/>
5. **SOL** — the EU's online dispute platform: <https://ec.europa.eu/consumers/odr>

Nothing on this page limits your legal rights. Where anything here conflicts with Romanian or EU consumer law, the law wins.
